Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

ClipForge is a private tool used by one person (the owner) to upload video clips to the owner's own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts. Contact: finn@roovers.biz.

Data we access

When the owner signs in with Google, ClipForge requests these permissions for the owner's own channels only:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel name and view counts of the owner's own videos.

When the owner logs in with TikTok, ClipForge requests these permissions for the owner's own TikTok accounts only:

  • user.info.basic: to show which of the owner's accounts is connected.
  • video.upload / video.publish: to send the owner's finished clips to the owner's own TikTok account (as a draft or as a post), with the caption the owner approved.

How data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload and check the owner's own videos.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • ClipForge does not access data of any other YouTube or TikTok user.

TikTok

ClipForge's use of TikTok APIs follows the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy. TikTok access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services; the stored tokens are then deleted.

Google API Services

ClipForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ClipForge uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner's computer. For questions or deletion requests, email finn@roovers.biz.
