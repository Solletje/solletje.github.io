ClipForge

ClipForge is a private, single-user tool. Its owner uses it to edit short video clips and upload them to the owner's own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts (Loud Chat Clips, VluggeClips, RepCuts, CageCuts and their TikTok counterparts).

What it does

  • Edits short vertical clips on the owner's own computer.
  • Uploads finished clips to the owner's own YouTube channels with the YouTube Data API, after the owner has signed in with Google and granted permission.
  • Sends finished clips to the owner's own TikTok accounts with the TikTok Content Posting API, after the owner has logged in with TikTok and approved the caption.
  • Reads basic channel information and video statistics (views) of those same channels.

ClipForge is not offered to the public, has no other users and does not access anyone else's account.
